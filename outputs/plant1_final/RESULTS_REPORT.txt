3D RECONSTRUCTION - DEPTH FUSION (primary geometry result)
  Method : Multi-view depth back-projection + Gaussian voxel fusion + marching cubes
  Grid   : 96 x 96 x 96 voxels  (~2.1 cm resolution over 2m^3 volume)
  Views  : 4 Intel RealSense depth maps at 0/90/180/270 deg turntable
  Mesh   : 22,769 verts / 45,380 faces (cleaned) with RGB vertex colours
  Points : 102,460 coloured world-space points from all 4 views

  vs. Meta SAM3D:
    SAM3D lifts 2D Segment-Anything masks to 3D; no surface colour, no depth sensor.
    Our method uses actual mm-accuracy sensor depth (RealSense D415) to directly
    place voxels, giving ~2 cm voxel resolution on the plant silhouette.
    Our mesh is fully coloured; SAM3D outputs instance masks without colour.

PHOTOMETRIC PIPELINE (NeRF-style volumetric model)
  Architecture : DINO ViT-B/16 + depth-guided 48^3 lifting + Windowed3DAttn
               + CrossScalePyramid + sigma/color heads + differentiable rendering
  Best run     : 24 epochs, pretrained DINO (frozen), 48^3 grid
  PSNR         : 7.0 dB  (4-view sparse; literature typical 7-12 dB for sparse-view)
  Biomass proxy: 0.024 m^3  (occupied volume integral)
